Evidence, not assumptions.

This standalone prototype uses a synthetic incident. No real AIS feed, satellite product, drift model or attribution engine is connected.

  • Oil track: timestamped demonstration positions, linearly interpolated. A reconstructed position marker moves before detection; the illustrative slick appears only at the observation time.
  • Source: an estimate at the first oil-track timestamp. It is distinct from the later observation.
  • Ocean: procedural visual waves, not a hydrodynamic simulation. Tracks, slick and markers sample the same water-height function.
  • Wind & current: illustrative arrows; they do not drive or calculate the oil path.
  • AIS: demonstration waypoints with course-derived heading. The tanker is a scale-exaggerated 3D mesh, not a verified vessel likeness.

Export includes demo inputs, timeline position, layers and camera state. Use the included source files to connect timestamped incident data.
INVESTIGATION CONTROLS

One scene. Every connection.

  • Time: drag the slider or use its arrow keys. Play replays the entire window in 45 seconds at 1×. Choose an event below the slider to jump to it.
  • Orbit: drag the ocean, or focus it and use arrow keys.
  • Pan: Shift + drag or right-button drag. On touchscreens, use two fingers.
  • Zoom: scroll, pinch, use + / − buttons or the + / − keys.
  • Views: Perspective, Top and Low presets. Reset restores the initial camera.
  • Layers: solid tracks show the past at the playhead; dashed tracks are full-window context. Switch context off for past-only playback.

Reduced-motion settings are respected. You can also disable animated waves without changing the incident timeline.
